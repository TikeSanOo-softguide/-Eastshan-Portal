# Translation review

These strings are usable, but a native reviewer should confirm the wording before they are treated as official.

- `common.appName`, `home.title` — Burmese “အရှေ့ရှမ်း” and Chinese “东山” assume Eastshan means eastern Shan. Replace them if the institution has a fixed official name.
- `nav.dashboard`, `admin.dashboardTitle` — Burmese “ထိန်းချုပ်ခန်း” and Chinese “控制台” describe the signed-in area. Keep the English word “Dashboard” if that is the product name.
- `home.styleGuide.outline`, `home.styleGuide.ghost` — Translated by what the button looks like (border / text), not by the English component names “Outline” and “Ghost”.
- `home.styleGuide.archive` — Burmese “မော်ကွန်း” and Chinese “档案”. Use “存档” in Chinese if this badge means “archived item” rather than “archives”.
- `common.rememberMe` — Burmese “ဒီကိရိယာမှာ မှတ်ထားပါ” and Chinese “在这台设备上记住我” say the device will remember the session. Shorten to “记住我” / “မှတ်ထားပါ” if a shorter label is preferred.
- `nav.dailyNews`, `nav.newsArticles` — Daily News is the short news list. News Articles is the longer written pieces. Confirm these labels stay distinct.
- `nav.health` — Chinese “医疗卫生” names health facilities. Use “卫生” if the item should be shorter in the menu.
- `footer.phone`, `footer.email`, `footer.address` — Invented sample contact details. Replace them before launch.
- `home.hero.title`, `home.hero.subtitle` — Public homepage voice. Confirm tone before launch; keep the “sample / placeholder” wording until copy is official.
- `nav.leadership`, `home.leadership.*` — “Leadership / 领导层 / ခေါင်းဆောင်မှု” for fictional sample profiles only.
